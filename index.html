BOAN is a female-only community interest company bringing women together through movement, nutrition and meaningful connection. We’re here to build confidence, open doors and make wellbeing feel within reach.

Cost, confidence, culture or caring responsibilities shouldn’t decide who gets to thrive. We’re working to remove those barriers, with welcoming opportunities shaped by women.

Who is BOAN for?+

BOAN is a female-only community interest company for women from different backgrounds, ages and stages of life. We particularly want to reach women who face barriers to mainstream wellbeing and community spaces.

Do I need any experience?+

No perfect starting point is needed. Explore Our Programs to see what interests you, or visit Contact Us to tell us what would help you feel welcome.

How can I help another woman take part?+

A monthly gift of £35 can support a woman to attend fitness classes for a month. You can explore single and monthly giving on our Support Our Work page.
