What is BOAN?+

BOAN stands for Builders Of A Nation. We're a female-only community interest company bringing fitness, nutrition and wellbeing together to create a welcoming hub for women.

Do I need to be into fitness already?+

No. You can start wherever you are. Movement is one part of BOAN, alongside nourishment, conversations and connection. Let us know what interests you and we'll help you find your way in.

How is SNB Hive involved?+

BOAN connects with SNB Hive for the fitness side of our community, bringing movement together with our wider focus on nutrition and wellbeing.

How can I get involved?+
