BOAN is a female-only community interest company bringing women together through movement, nutrition and meaningful connection. We’re here to build confidence, open doors and make wellbeing feel within reach.

Cost, confidence, culture or caring responsibilities shouldn’t decide who gets to thrive. We’re working to remove those barriers, with welcoming opportunities shaped by the women who use them.

YOUR FIRST STEP STARTS HERE

A few questions.
A warm welcome.

Who is BOAN for?+

BOAN is a female-only community interest company for women from different backgrounds, ages and stages of life. We particularly want to reach women who face barriers to mainstream wellbeing and community spaces.

Do I need any experience to join in?+

No. Start wherever you are. Movement, nutrition, conversations and workshops offer different ways to get involved. Tell us what interests you and what would help you feel welcome.

How is SNB Hive involved?+

BOAN connects with SNB Hive for the fitness side of our community, bringing movement together with our wider focus on nutrition and wellbeing.

Are free or subsidised activities available?+

Making activities accessible is central to our mission. We work with partners and funders to support free or subsidised opportunities where possible. Confirmed activities, availability and costs will be shared when programmes open.

How can I get involved?+

Choose “Find your people” to explore your interests. This website is a preview: you can download a personal note of interest, but nothing is submitted online and bookings are not open yet.
