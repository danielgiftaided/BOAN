We’re Builders Of A Nation: a female-only community interest company working to make wellbeing, movement and meaningful connection more accessible.

WHY WE EXIST

Wellbeing should feel
within reach.

For many women, taking part is about more than finding a class. Cost, confidence, caring responsibilities, culture, privacy needs and a lack of suitable local spaces can all stand in the way.

BOAN brings together people, programmes, local spaces and partnerships to help remove those barriers. We want women to feel welcome, build confidence and strengthen connections in their community.

Our work is community-led. We listen to what women tell us they need, then work with others to create welcoming opportunities that can grow with them.

01 / BELONGING

A space to be yourself.

Different backgrounds, ages and stages of life. One community where women feel welcome, represented and connected.

02 / OPPORTUNITY

Room to grow.

New skills, stronger confidence and the support to take your next step, whatever that looks like for you.

03 / ACCESS

Fewer barriers.

We work with partners to widen access, including free and subsidised opportunities where funding makes them possible.

HOW WE BUILD TOGETHER

Listen. Connect.
Enable. Grow.

  1. Listen

    Understand what local women need and what stops them taking part.

  2. Connect

    Bring together spaces, practitioners, partners and community networks.

  3. Enable

    Use funding and partnership support to reduce cost and practical barriers.

  4. Grow

    Learn from participation and feedback to develop sustainable opportunities.
