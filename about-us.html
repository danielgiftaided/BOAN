Builders of a Nation CIC (BOAN) is a community-led organisation dedicated to empowering women, strengthening communities and creating opportunities for women to connect, grow and thrive.

A place to belong.
A future to build.

Founded on the belief that strong communities begin with supported, confident and empowered individuals, BOAN aims to break down barriers, tackle social isolation and improve access to wellbeing, education and personal development opportunities. We believe that everyone deserves a safe, welcoming and inclusive environment where they feel valued, supported and inspired to reach their full potential.

Our work focuses on bringing women together through health and wellbeing activities, educational programmes, creative workshops, social initiatives and community events. By encouraging meaningful connections, building confidence and developing new skills, we aim to create lasting positive change in the lives of individuals and the wider community.

At the heart of our vision is the creation of a dedicated community hub in Hackney — a welcoming space where women of all ages, cultures and backgrounds can come together, access opportunities, share experiences and find a genuine sense of belonging.

BOAN is about more than delivering programmes. It's about building a community where women support one another, opportunities are accessible, and everyone has the chance to flourish. Together, we can build stronger women, stronger families and stronger communities.

Our Mission

To remove the financial, social and practical barriers that prevent women from accessing high-quality wellbeing experiences, opportunities for personal growth and meaningful community connections.

At Builders of a Nation CIC (BOAN), we are bold, ambitious and determined to create lasting change. We are committed to creating and supporting safe, welcoming and inclusive spaces where women of all ages and backgrounds can build confidence, improve their wellbeing, develop new skills and form meaningful relationships.

We believe every woman deserves the opportunity to thrive, regardless of her background or circumstances.
