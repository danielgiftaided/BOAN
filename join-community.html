Your wellbeing
starts here.

Perhaps you want to feel stronger, try something new or simply have time for yourself. You might be returning to exercise, rebuilding your confidence or unsure where to begin.

Whatever your starting point, you deserve to feel supported. BOAN brings women together through movement, learning, creativity and connection—with a focus on making these opportunities accessible to women facing barriers.

Find something
that feels right for you.

Wellbeing looks different for everyone. Our programmes are being developed to offer different ways to take that first step:

  • Movement

    Welcoming fitness activities to help you build strength, confidence and a routine you enjoy.

  • Workshops

    Practical sessions exploring women’s health, financial wellbeing and personal development.

  • Creative experiences

    Opportunities to unwind, learn something new and connect with other women.

  • Retreats

    Time and space to rest, reflect and focus on your wellbeing.

Activities, dates and availability will be shared as they are confirmed. Registering your interest helps us understand what women in our community want and need.

When barriers
stand in your way.

Cost, low confidence, caring responsibilities or uncertainty about where to start can make taking part feel difficult.

We’re working to make that first step easier. As funding becomes available, BOAN aims to offer funded or subsidised places for women who would otherwise struggle to access our activities.

Financial support will be means-tested, subject to eligibility and available funding. We’ll explain the criteria and ask only for information needed to assess your application, treating your circumstances with care and respect.

You’re welcome to register your interest even if you’re unsure whether you qualify.

Is BOAN
for me?

Our community welcomes women from all backgrounds. You don’t need previous experience, a particular fitness level or someone to come with you.

You may be looking to become more active, reconnect with others, learn new skills or make space for yourself. You can start with a conversation about what interests you and what would help you take part.

Our initial focus is on women in Hackney. Individual programmes may have specific age, location or funding criteria, which we’ll make clear before you apply.

Take your
first step.

  1. Tell us a little about yourself.

    Complete our short interest form with your contact details, the activities you’re interested in and anything that would make joining easier.

  2. We’ll explain the options.

    As suitable opportunities become available, we’ll contact you with details. If you’re seeking financial support, we’ll explain the eligibility assessment and any information needed.

  3. Find your way into the community.

    Once a suitable place is confirmed, we’ll share what to expect and help you prepare for your first session.

Registering your interest does not guarantee a place or funding, but it helps us keep you informed about relevant opportunities.

A few questions
before you begin.

Do I need to be fit or have experience?+

No. Beginners are welcome. We’ll explain the level and requirements of each activity so you can decide whether it feels suitable for you.

Can I come on my own?+

Yes. You don’t need to bring a friend or know anyone beforehand. We want women joining for the first time to feel welcome.

Are all activities free?+

Not necessarily. Some opportunities may be funded or subsidised, while others may involve a contribution. We’ll explain costs before you commit. Financial support depends on eligibility and available funding.

What if I have a health condition, disability or accessibility need?+

Please contact us to discuss what would help you participate. We’ll talk through the activity, any available adjustments and whether the provider can meet your needs.

Will my financial circumstances stay private?+

Information used to assess financial support will be limited to those who need it to assess or administer your application. Your financial circumstances will not be shared with donors. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

What happens if nothing suitable is available yet?+

With your permission, we’ll keep your details so we can contact you when relevant opportunities become available.
