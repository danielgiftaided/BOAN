Our Programmes

At Builders of a Nation CIC (BOAN), we believe every woman deserves access to high-quality experiences that support her wellbeing, personal growth and sense of belonging.

Our programmes are designed to remove financial, social and practical barriers, creating opportunities for women of all ages and backgrounds to connect, learn, grow and thrive.

MovementWorkshopsCreativeRetreats

Movement

Supporting physical and mental wellbeing through accessible, engaging activities such as Pilates, yoga, strength training, dance and fitness. Our movement programmes encourage women to build confidence, improve their health and discover the benefits of an active lifestyle in a welcoming and supportive environment.

Ask about movement

Workshops

Empowering women through informative, engaging and practical workshops that address important aspects of everyday life. From women's health and wellbeing topics such as menopause, hormonal health and mental wellbeing, to financial education covering budgeting, saving, investing and building financial independence, our workshops aim to provide women with the knowledge, confidence and tools to make informed decisions.

We also explore personal development, self-defence and essential life skills, creating safe spaces for open conversations, shared experiences and learning from experienced professionals.

Ask about workshops

Creative

Encouraging self-expression, creativity and connection through activities such as painting, flower arranging, crocheting, knitting and other hands-on experiences. Our creative sessions provide a welcoming space for women to explore new interests, discover talents, unwind and build meaningful friendships.

Ask about creative

Retreats

Offering thoughtfully curated wellbeing experiences that allow women to step away from everyday responsibilities and focus on themselves. Through relaxation, movement, nature and meaningful connection, our retreats provide opportunities to recharge, reflect and restore balance in inspiring and nurturing environments.

Ask about retreats

Across every programme, our purpose remains the same: to make enriching experiences accessible, strengthen connections and create spaces where every woman feels valued, supported and empowered.
