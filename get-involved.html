Get Involved

At Builders of a Nation CIC (BOAN), we believe meaningful change happens when people come together with a shared purpose. Whether you're an individual, community organisation or business, there are many ways to support our mission and make a lasting difference.

We welcome those who share our vision of removing barriers, creating opportunities and building stronger, more connected communities.

Volunteer With Us

Give your time, skills and experience to help us create welcoming spaces and deliver meaningful activities. Whether supporting events, programmes or behind-the-scenes operations, your contribution can make a real difference.

Talk about volunteering

Partner With Us

We believe collaboration is key to creating lasting impact. We welcome partnerships with businesses, local authorities, community organisations and other stakeholders who share our commitment to improving women's wellbeing and access to opportunities.

Explore a partnership

Become an Ambassador

Help us spread the word, connect with local communities and inspire more women to get involved. Our ambassadors play an important role in raising awareness, building relationships and extending our reach.
