Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Builders Of A Nation CIC (BOAN) respects your privacy. We are committed to treating your personal information carefully, fairly and with respect.

This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you visit our website, contact us, apply for support, take part in our activities or support our work.

1. Who we are

Builders Of A Nation CIC is the organisation responsible for the personal information covered by this policy, known as the “data controller”.

Company number: 17432415
Email: hello@boan.uk
Website: https://boan.uk

2. Information we collect

Depending on your involvement with BOAN, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number and postcode.
  • Information you provide through enquiries, applications and correspondence.
  • Your interests, bookings, attendance and feedback.
  • Information about your circumstances needed to assess eligibility for funded or subsidised places, such as household income or receipt of benefits.
  • Relevant health, disability or accessibility information where needed to support your participation.
  • Donation details, including amounts, dates and transaction references.
  • Information about volunteering, ambassador roles or partnerships.
  • Your communication preferences and any permissions you give us.
  • Technical information such as your IP address, browser type and website security logs.

We collect only information relevant to the purpose. Please do not send financial documents or detailed medical information through our general enquiry form.

3. How we receive information

We usually receive information directly from you through our website, email, application forms, conversations and activities.

We may also receive relevant information from an organisation referring you to us, a delivery partner or a payment provider. Where someone makes a referral, they should ensure you know your information is being shared. We will explain how we use information received about you.

4. Why we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests.
  • Register your interest and arrange participation in activities.
  • Assess applications for funded or subsidised support.
  • Understand accessibility needs and make appropriate arrangements.
  • Manage donations, volunteering and partnerships.
  • Send practical information about activities you have requested or booked.
  • Send news and fundraising updates where you have opted in.
  • Evaluate our programmes and prepare reports for funders.
  • Manage complaints, protect participants and keep our systems secure.
  • Meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Where possible, reporting uses anonymous information that does not identify individual participants.

5. Our lawful bases

Under UK data protection law, we must have a lawful basis for using your information.

We rely on:

  • Legitimate interests: to respond to enquiries, administer applications and community programmes, maintain supporter relationships, evaluate our work and protect our systems. We consider your rights and reasonable expectations before relying on this basis.
  • Contract: where information is necessary to arrange or deliver a service you have booked, or take steps you request before entering an agreement.
  • Consent: for optional marketing communications and the publication of identifiable photographs, testimonials or personal stories.
  • Legal obligation: where we must retain or disclose information to comply with the law, including accounting requirements.

Health information and certain other sensitive information have additional protection. Where we request this information to support participation, we will explain why and seek your explicit consent where that is the appropriate legal condition. If another condition is necessary, we will explain it in the relevant notice.

6. Applying for funded or subsidised support

Some opportunities may be subject to eligibility criteria, available funding and an assessment of your circumstances.

We will explain what information is required and why. If evidence is needed, we will tell you how to provide it securely. You may obscure unrelated information where it is not needed for the assessment.

Access to eligibility information will be limited to people who need it to assess or administer support. We do not share your financial circumstances with donors.

You can choose not to provide information, but we may be unable to assess your eligibility or arrange suitable support without it. We will explain this when asking.

7. Who we share information with

Where necessary, we may share relevant information with:

  • Authorised BOAN team members and volunteers.
  • Instructors, venues and delivery partners involved in an activity you attend.
  • Providers of website hosting, forms, email, secure storage and payment services.
  • Professional advisers, insurers or public authorities where necessary and lawful.

We share only what is needed for the particular purpose. Instructors would not normally need access to financial evidence used for an eligibility assessment.

Where an activity is delivered by SNB Hive or another organisation, we will explain who is responsible for your information and provide any relevant privacy notice before sharing it.

We do not sell your personal information. Identifiable participant stories or photographs will only be used for publicity with appropriate permission.

8. Donations

When online donations become available, payment information will be processed by the payment provider identified at checkout.

We may retain donation and transaction records for administration, accounting and legal purposes. Making a donation does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails.

9. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for its purpose.

When deciding how long to retain it, we consider:

  • Whether an enquiry or application remains active.
  • The duration of your involvement and any necessary follow-up.
  • Funding agreements and audit requirements.
  • Accounting, insurance and other legal requirements.
  • The need to resolve complaints or establish or defend legal claims.

Financial evidence and health information are reviewed separately and should not be retained simply because we retain an attendance or donation record.

We securely delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed. You can contact us for details of the retention period applying to your records.

10. Keeping information secure

We use appropriate organisational and technical measures to protect personal information, including restricting access to people who need it.

No online system is completely secure. If you need to share sensitive information, please contact us first so we can explain the appropriate method.

11. Cookies and website technology

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functions and other purposes.

Where consent is required, we will ask before using the relevant technology.

12. Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Obtain a copy of your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Ask us to delete information.
  • Ask us to restrict how we use it.
  • Receive certain information in a reusable electronic format.
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exemptions.

Your right to object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can also object to direct marketing at any time.

To exercise your rights, email hello@boan.uk. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

You can unsubscribe from marketing messages using the link provided or by emailing us. We may still send essential administrative messages about an application, booking or donation.

13. Children and young people

Where a programme involves children or young people, we will provide age-appropriate privacy information and explain any parent or guardian involvement required.

We will collect only the information necessary to organise and deliver the activity safely and appropriately.

14. Questions and complaints

If you have a question or concern about your information, contact hello@boan.uk.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), whether or not you have contacted us first:

Website: https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services develop. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date. Where a change significantly affects how we use your information, we will take appropriate steps to let you know.
