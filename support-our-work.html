What can £35 a month support?+

For £35 a month, you can support a woman to attend fitness classes for a month. It helps remove a financial barrier so she can take part in movement and connect with the community.

Can I make a single donation instead?+

Yes. Choose “Single donation” above to explore a one-off amount. Both single and monthly support will help BOAN widen access to its work when online giving opens.

Can I donate online now?+

Not yet. This is a preview of the donation page. You can explore amounts and review your choice, but no payment details are collected, no money is taken and no monthly donation is set up.

Can my organisation help?+

We welcome conversations about funding, sponsorship, venues, expertise and other practical support. Visit Contact Us to explore a partnership.
